# Darius Game

Darius Game is a 2D pixel platformer developed in Unity 6.

The player controls a superhero named DariusMan. The main goal is to collect coins, defeat enemies, avoid obstacles, and complete different stages.

## Features

- Three playable campaign levels
- Boss fight with ShadowKing
- Enemy AI Arena with three unique enemy types
- Player movement and jumping
- Shooting system
- Enemy combat
- Coin collection
- Health system
- Main menu and stage selection
- Pause menu and settings
- Mobile joystick and buttons
- Sound effects and background music
- Victory and game-over screens

## Game Stages

### Stage 1 — Campaign

The main campaign includes three levels and a boss fight.

#### Level 1 — Green Valley

The first level introduces basic movement and jumping. The player must collect 8 coins and reach the exit gate.

#### Level 2 — Desert Canyon

The second level includes enemies, falling rocks, and more difficult obstacles. The player must collect 10 coins to unlock the exit gate.

#### Level 3 — Dark Castle

The third level includes moving platforms, falling bombs, and stronger enemies. The player must collect 12 coins and reach the exit gate.

### Boss Fight — ShadowKing

ShadowKing is the main boss of Darius Game.

The player must avoid his attacks and use shooting to defeat him.

### Stage 2 — Enemy AI Arena

Enemy AI Arena is a separate stage featuring three different enemy types:

- **Patroller** — Patrols the area, chases the player, and attacks at close range.
- **Shooter** — Detects the player and attacks with projectiles.
- **Charger** — Prepares a powerful charging attack toward the player.

The player must survive enemy attacks, defeat enemies, collect coins, and unlock the exit gate.

## Controls

### PC

- A / D or Arrow Keys — Move
- Space — Jump
- X or Left Mouse Button — Shoot
- ESC — Pause

### Mobile

- Joystick — Move
- JUMP Button — Jump
- FIRE Button — Shoot
- MENU Button — Pause
